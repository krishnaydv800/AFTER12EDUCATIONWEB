✨ ABOUT AFTER12

Helping students make better decisions after Class 12.

After12 is a frontend education-guide concept designed to organize information about courses, careers, colleges and exams.

OUR MISSION

Make career exploration simple.

After Class 12, students often hear many different suggestions. Our platform brings the basic questions into one place.

What can I study? What careers can follow? Which exams matter? What should I check before choosing a college?

The goal is not to tell every student what to choose. It is to help students ask better questions and make informed decisions.

🚀 Plan Turn research into action.
