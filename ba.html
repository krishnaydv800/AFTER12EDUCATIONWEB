COURSE OVERVIEW

What is BA?

Bachelor of Arts is an undergraduate program offering students the opportunity to study subjects from humanities, social sciences, languages and related areas.

BA can be a flexible option for students interested in civil services, teaching, law, media, psychology, public policy, research and many other fields.

Quick Information

FAQ

BA Questions

Is BA a good course?

BA can be a strong choice when its subjects match your interests and career plans. The value also depends on the skills, internships and further education you pursue.

Can Science students do BA?

Many institutions offer BA programs to students from different Class 12 streams, but eligibility varies by university and subject.

Can I prepare for government exams after BA?

Yes. A bachelor's degree is an eligibility component for many graduate-level examinations, while each exam has its own specific requirements.

Can I do MA after BA?

Yes. MA is a common postgraduate pathway, subject to the eligibility requirements of the university and chosen specialization.
