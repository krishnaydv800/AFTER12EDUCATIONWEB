COURSE OVERVIEW

What is BBA?

BBA is an undergraduate management program designed to introduce students to business administration, marketing, finance, human resources and organizational management.

It can be a suitable choice for students who want to understand how companies operate and are interested in management or entrepreneurship.

FAQ

BBA Questions

BBA or B.Com — which should I choose?

BBA generally emphasizes business administration and management, while B.Com emphasizes commerce, accounting and finance. Choose based on your preferred subjects and career direction.

Can I do MBA after BBA?

Yes. BBA provides a relevant undergraduate foundation for MBA and other management postgraduate programs.

Is BBA useful for entrepreneurship?

BBA can introduce you to business models, marketing, finance and management. Real-world experience and practical skills are equally important.

Does BBA require Mathematics?

Requirements vary between universities and programs. Check the eligibility criteria of the institution.
