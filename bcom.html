COURSE OVERVIEW

What is B.Com?

B.Com is an undergraduate commerce program covering accounting, economics, business, taxation, finance, management and related subjects.

It is suitable for students interested in business, finance, banking, accounting and entrepreneurship.

Quick Information

Duration Usually 3–4 years
Level Undergraduate
Stream Commerce
Field Commerce & Finance
ELIGIBILITY

Who should consider B.Com?

🎓 Class 12

Students completing Class 12 with the required subjects can consider B.Com.

A genuine interest in business, accounts and finance is helpful.

🧮 Mathematics

Mathematics requirements vary between universities and programs.

📝 Admission

Admission may be based on merit or an entrance process depending on the institution.

FAQ

B.Com Questions

Is B.Com only for commerce students?

Eligibility differs by institution. Some programs may accept students from other streams if their criteria are satisfied.

Can I do MBA after B.Com?

Yes. MBA is a common postgraduate option after B.Com, subject to the admission requirements of the institute.

Can I prepare for CA with B.Com?

Yes. B.Com can be pursued alongside preparation for professional commerce qualifications, subject to their respective rules.

Is B.Com good for banking?

B.Com provides useful foundations in accounting, finance and business, which can be relevant for banking-related careers and examinations.
