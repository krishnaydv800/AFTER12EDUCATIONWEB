What is B.Sc?

Bachelor of Science is an undergraduate program in scientific disciplines such as Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science and other specialized fields.

The exact subjects, combinations and duration depend on the university and specialization.

FAQ

B.Sc Questions

Is B.Sc only for students who want research?

No. Depending on the specialization and skills developed, graduates can explore research, teaching, industry, technology and other career directions.

B.Sc or B.Tech — which is better?

They are different pathways. B.Tech is an engineering and technology degree, while B.Sc generally provides deeper study of a scientific discipline. Choose according to your interests and career goals.

Can I do M.Sc after B.Sc?

Yes, M.Sc is a common postgraduate pathway, subject to the eligibility requirements of the chosen university.
