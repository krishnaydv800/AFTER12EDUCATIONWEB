COURSE OVERVIEW

What is B.Tech?

B.Tech is an undergraduate engineering and technology program for students interested in engineering, technology, problem solving and innovation.

Students can choose different branches such as Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil and Electrical Engineering.

Quick Information

Duration Usually 4 years
Level Undergraduate
Stream Science
Field Engineering & Technology
ELIGIBILITY

Who can choose B.Tech?

📚 Class 12

Students generally need the relevant Class 12 subjects as specified by the institution.

🧮 Mathematics

Mathematics is commonly required for many engineering programs.

🔬 Science

Physics and other relevant science subjects are commonly part of eligibility.

📝 Entrance

Admission may involve entrance exams or institute-specific processes.
