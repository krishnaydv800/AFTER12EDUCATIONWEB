📘 What is CUET?

CUET stands for Common University Entrance Test. CUET-UG is an entrance examination used by participating universities for undergraduate admissions.

The examination provides students with a common platform through which they can seek admission to participating universities and their eligible undergraduate programs.

The subjects a student should select depend on the course and university requirements. Therefore, students should check the eligibility and subject combination required for their target programs.

🎯 Why is CUET conducted?

  • To provide a common entrance examination route for undergraduate admissions.
  • To allow participating universities to use CUET scores in their admission process.
  • To provide students with opportunities to apply for multiple participating institutions.
  • To assess candidates through specified language, domain and/or general test components as applicable.

🎓 CUET Eligibility

CUET eligibility can depend on the university, course and admission year. Candidates must check the requirements of each program they want to apply for.

Basic Qualification

  • Candidates generally need to have passed or be appearing in Class 12/equivalent qualification.
  • The required subjects can differ according to the selected undergraduate program.
  • Minimum marks and other conditions may be specified by individual universities.

Important

Qualifying for CUET does not automatically guarantee admission to every participating university or program. The candidate must satisfy the individual program eligibility requirements.

🏫 Universities & Admission

CUET scores are used by participating universities according to their respective admission policies.

Admission Process Generally Includes

  1. Appear for CUET.
  2. Receive examination result/score.
  3. Check participating university requirements.
  4. Apply/register for the applicable admission process.
  5. Fill choices or preferences where required.
  6. Participate in the university's admission process.
  7. Complete document verification.
  8. Pay admission fees and report to the institution.

The exact process is university and program specific.

⚠️ Common CUET Mistakes

  • Choosing subjects without checking university requirements.
  • Ignoring individual course eligibility.
  • Preparing only for the exam and not checking admission requirements.
  • Not practicing objective questions.
  • Ignoring time management.
  • Not reading the official notification.
  • Applying without checking the final form carefully.
  • Following outdated syllabus information.

❓ CUET Frequently Asked Questions

What is the full form of CUET?

CUET stands for Common University Entrance Test.

What is CUET-UG?

CUET-UG is the Common University Entrance Test for undergraduate admissions in participating universities.

Can CUET be used for BCA?

Some participating universities offer BCA or related programs through CUET. The exact eligibility and subject requirements must be checked for the university and course.

Can CUET be used for BBA?

Some participating universities offer BBA or related management programs through CUET. Always check the current program requirements.

Is CUET compulsory for every university?

No. CUET applies to participating universities and their applicable programs. Students should check the admission rules of their target institution.

Is CUET an online exam?

CUET is conducted in computer-based examination mode, subject to the current examination arrangements.
