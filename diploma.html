FAQ

Diploma Questions

Is diploma better than a degree?

They serve different purposes. Diplomas often focus more directly on practical skills, while degrees generally provide broader academic education.

Can I get a job after a diploma?

Some diploma programs are designed for entry-level employment, but opportunities depend on the field, institution, skill level and employer requirements.

Can I study further after a diploma?

In many fields there may be pathways to further education, but eligibility and lateral-entry rules depend on the program and institution.
