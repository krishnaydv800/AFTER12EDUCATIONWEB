COURSE OVERVIEW

What is an Integrated Law Course?

Integrated law programs combine an undergraduate degree with legal education in a single program. Common formats include BA LL.B and BBA LL.B.

These programs are suitable for students who are interested in law, legal systems, advocacy, corporate legal work and public service.

FAQ

Law Questions

Can I study law after Class 12?

Yes. Integrated undergraduate law programs are designed for students entering legal education after Class 12.

Do I need to study only Arts for law?

No. Students from different Class 12 streams can be eligible for law programs depending on the institution.

What entrance exams can be relevant for law?

Entrance requirements vary by institution. Some law universities conduct their own or centralized entrance examinations.
