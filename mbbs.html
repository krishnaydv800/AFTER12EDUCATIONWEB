MBBS

Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery.

COURSE OVERVIEW

What is MBBS?

MBBS is a professional medical degree that prepares students for the practice of medicine through theoretical learning, laboratory work and clinical training.

Medical education requires strong commitment, disciplined study and extensive practical and clinical learning.

Quick Information

Duration About 5.5 years including internship
Level Undergraduate Medical
Stream Science / PCB
Entrance NEET-UG based admission in India
ELIGIBILITY

Who can pursue MBBS?

🔬 PCB

Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Biotechnology are central subjects for medical eligibility.

🎓 Class 12

Candidates must satisfy the applicable Class 12 eligibility requirements.

📝 Entrance Exam

Admission to undergraduate medical programs in India is generally through NEET-UG and the applicable counselling process.

❤️ Commitment

Medicine requires patience, discipline, empathy and willingness to study continuously.

FAQ

MBBS Questions

Which entrance exam is required for MBBS in India?

Undergraduate medical admission in India is generally based on NEET-UG along with the applicable counselling and admission rules.

Is MBBS difficult?

It requires consistent study, practical learning and clinical training. Students should be prepared for a demanding academic schedule.

Can I specialize after MBBS?

Yes. Medical graduates can pursue postgraduate medical education and specialization subject to applicable eligibility and admission processes.

Should I choose MBBS only because of salary?

Medicine is a long-term professional commitment. Interest in healthcare, patient care and the nature of the work should be important factors in your decision.
