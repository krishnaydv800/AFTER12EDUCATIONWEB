📘 What is NEET?

NEET stands for National Eligibility cum Entrance Test. It is the major national-level entrance examination for undergraduate medical education in India.

Students who want to pursue medical education such as MBBS and other participating programs generally need to qualify through the applicable NEET admission process.

NEET is conducted at the national level and the result is used in counselling and admission processes according to the applicable rules.

🎯 Why is NEET conducted?

  • To provide a common entrance examination route for undergraduate medical admissions.
  • To assess students' understanding of Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  • To support centralized and state-level counselling processes according to applicable rules.
  • To provide an admission pathway for participating medical institutions and programs.

🎓 NEET Eligibility

Eligibility requirements are determined according to the current examination notification and applicable admission rules.

Educational Qualification

  • Candidates generally need the required Class 12 or equivalent qualification.
  • Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Biotechnology are important qualifying subjects.
  • Candidates appearing in the qualifying examination may also be eligible under applicable rules.

Age Requirement

Age-related eligibility should always be checked in the latest official NEET notification because rules can be updated.

🚀 NEET Preparation Strategy

01

Start with NCERT

Build your Biology and Chemistry foundation using the prescribed school-level material and the current syllabus.

02

Make a Study Schedule

Divide your time between Physics, Chemistry and Biology and maintain a realistic daily routine.

03

Practice Questions

Solve objective questions regularly and identify the concepts where you are making mistakes.

04

Previous-Year Questions

Practice previous-year questions to understand the style and level of questions.

05

Mock Tests

Take timed mock tests and practice completing the paper efficiently.

06

Revision

Keep revising formulas, diagrams, reactions and important concepts.

❓ NEET Frequently Asked Questions

What is the full form of NEET?

NEET stands for National Eligibility cum Entrance Test.

Which subjects are required for NEET?

The major subjects are Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Biotechnology.

Is NEET required for MBBS admission?

NEET is the principal national entrance route for undergraduate medical admissions in India, subject to the applicable admission rules.

Can Class 12 students apply?

Students appearing in the qualifying examination may be eligible under the applicable rules.

Is Biology important for NEET?

Yes. Biology is a major part of the examination, so strong conceptual understanding and regular revision are important.

Should I solve previous-year questions?

Yes. PYQs help you understand the question style, difficulty and topics that need more practice.
